# Wristbands and Gloves

Wristbands and Gloves are Curios hand equipment designed for empty-hand fighters. They let an unarmed character use equipment bonuses and weapon enchantments without visually holding a weapon.

## Equipping handwear

Place either item in the Curios **hands** slot. Simply carrying it, holding it directly, or placing it in a different Curios slot does not activate its special bonuses.

Handwear is active only while the main hand is completely empty. Equipping any item in the main hand immediately disables its empty-hand stat bonus, enchantments, affixes, and gems. Removing that item activates the handwear again.

## Stat bonuses

- Wristbands grant a 5% multiplicative STR bonus named **Empty Hand Bonus**.
- Gloves grant a 5% multiplicative PWR bonus named **Empty Hand Bonus**.

The bonus appears as a Dragon Mine Z bonus and is synchronized whenever the main-hand state changes. Unequipping the handwear or holding another item removes the corresponding bonus.

## Enchantments and durability

Both items can receive most enchantments that are valid for a sword. Sweeping Edge is excluded.

While active, relevant weapon effects are applied to empty-hand attacks, including damage enchantments, Fire Aspect, Knockback, Looting, and post-attack enchantment hooks. Every successful use damages the equipped handwear as a weapon would, including normal Unbreaking behavior.

When compatible attribute systems are installed, supported sword affixes and gems on the handwear are also read from the Curios slot. They follow the same empty-main-hand requirement.

## Crafting

Gloves are crafted from leather and white dye. Wristbands are crafted from blue wool. Their recipes are available through the normal recipe viewer when the required items have been discovered.
